Drag to spin. This demo uses computer-generated frames so you can feel the interaction — the real version will use photos of your actual product.

↻ Drag to rotate

How the real one works: a 360° spin is just a sequence of photos shown one after another as you drag — exactly like this demo, but with real pictures of your cat tree.

To make it for a real product:

  1. Put the product on something that turns (a lazy-susan / turntable, or a stool you rotate).
  2. Keep your phone still on a tripod, same distance, even lighting, plain background.
  3. Take about 30–36 photos, rotating the product a little between each until it's gone all the way around.
  4. Send me that folder — I'll drop them into this exact viewer.
